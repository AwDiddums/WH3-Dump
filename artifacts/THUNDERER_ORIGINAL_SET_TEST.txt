Thunderer original-animation-set test

This is a DIAGNOSTIC build, not an in-game-verified fix.
The previous merge contained the intended reload entries. The uploaded user
export also had both Thunderer rows assigned correctly. A specific runtime
cause for the absent reload has not been established.

This test takes the original marthreload animation container as its baseline.
It preserves all four reload entries and every other source slot, except five
empty jumping-attack entries (489-493) replaced by the texture-fix melee attacks.
The other four non-Dwarf fragments in @marthreload.animpack are byte-identical.
It changes standard Thunderers back to the vanilla hu3_dwarf_axe_shield_rifle
name in BOTH land_units table files found in the uploaded export. All other
row values, unit stats and units are preserved exactly from that export.
Actual Dwarf reload/ramrod and the five melee attack assets are included.

Installation:
1. Back up your FULL compatibility pack, including its current land_units tables.
2. In RPFM import ALL contents of awd_thunderer_original_set_test_IMPORT.pack
   into that full pack, allowing the two matching land_units files to replace
   their existing copies. These files are based on the supplied diagnostic export;
   preserve any newer edits before importing if you changed them since that export.
3. Keep the original Reloading Animations and texture/model fixes enabled.
4. Test standard Thunderers standing still and firing several volleys. Also test
   their melee charge/jumping attacks. Their man_animation should now be
   hu3_dwarf_axe_shield_rifle in this diagnostic setup.

Do not replace your full mod with this small import pack. Other rifle unit rows
are unchanged. Your custom dwf_axe_shield_fix.animpack is also unchanged and
continues to serve the other unit mappings. Registration-test rows can remain;
the standard Thunderer row in this test no longer selects their custom name.

No crossbow reload clips exist in either supplied source pack, so no crossbow
reload animation is added. Grudge-Rakers and Malakai already use reload clips
present in the texture-fix animation sets and are unchanged by this test.

To undo, restore your backup, or restore the two land_units files and remove the
imported @marthreload.animpack from your compatibility pack. Leave the original
Reloading Animations pack intact.
